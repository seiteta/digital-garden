## Why do we need an ai/score?
With AI becoming mainstream, many people want to know if the text they're reading was written by a human, or generated by an AI.

Human-written and AI-generated texts both have pros and cons.
Just like handmade ceramics versus manufactured tableware.

Sometimes, you crave for the beauty of a Japanese handcrafted teacup.
Other times, you need a hundred Ikea glasses because you invited all your friends to your birthday.

Likewise, sometimes you need an [authentic voice](https://maggieappleton.com/ai-dark-forest), filled with flaws and emotion.
And sometimes, you prefer a robotic message that always [meet spec](https://seths.blog/2020/05/meeting-spec-doing-the-minimum/).

The ai/score exists to help readers wandering "Was this written by a human or generated by an AI?"


## The 6 items of the ai/score

The ai/score goes from `ai/0` to `ai/5`, from full-human to full-AI. The six items are the following;

- [ai/0](ai-0): This text was created by a human, without any AI intervention.
- [ai/1](ai-1): This text was created by a human and lightly edited by an AI.
- [ai/2](ai-2): This text was created by a human and heavily edited by an AI.
- [ai/3](ai-3): This text was generated by an AI and heavily edited by a human.
- [ai/4](ai-4): This text was generated by an AI and lightly edited by a human.
- [ai/5](ai-5): This text was generated by an AI, without any human intervention.

## FAQ 

**Q: How to use the ai/score?**

A: Simply add `ai/x` at the beginning or the end of your content.
Now your readers know if it was written, generated, or something in between.

**Q: How can I know if an ai/score reflects the truth?**

A: All pre-2022 content can be considered `ai/0`.
After that, you will have to trust the content producer.

**Q: Who's gonna mention their texts are `ai/5` anyway?**

A: Short answer: user interfaces.
Example: a company using a chatbot on its website can add an `ai/5` mention. This way, its customers know their talking to an AI, not a human.

## Other initiative
Shortly after writing this article, I discovered another initiative: [Authorship Indicators: A Manifesto](https://authorshipindicators.org/).
The name of the initiative isn't very inspired, but their scale is very complete, ranging from 0 to 9:

- [AI:0 - Fully Human-Made](https://authorshipindicators.org/scorecards/aim-0/)
- [AI:1 - AI as Polisher](https://authorshipindicators.org/scorecards/aim-1/)
- [AI:2 - AI as Verifier](https://authorshipindicators.org/scorecards/aim-2/)
- [AI:3 - AI as Stylist](https://authorshipindicators.org/scorecards/aim-3/)
- [AI:4 - AI as Architect](https://authorshipindicators.org/scorecards/aim-4/)
- [AI:5 - AI as Co-Author](https://authorshipindicators.org/scorecards/aim-5/)
- [AI:6 - AI as Primary Creator](https://authorshipindicators.org/scorecards/aim-6/)
- [AI:7 - AI as Lead Creator](https://authorshipindicators.org/scorecards/aim-7/)
- [AI:8 - AI as Sole Creator](https://authorshipindicators.org/scorecards/aim-8/)
- [AI:9 - Fully Machine-Generated](https://authorshipindicators.org/scorecards/aim-9/)

They also include nice donut charts to illustrate each item on the scale. It's funny to see how close the name of our scales are: ai/number vs AI:number. The idea must have been floating in the noosphere.

Anyway, I'm keeping my ai/Score instead of their AI:M Score, because we never have enough standards…
