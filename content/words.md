### [Nadir](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nadir)
The opposite of zenith aka when the sun is at his lowest.

### [Numeronym](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Numeronym)
A word composed partially or wholly of numerals. Example: k8s (for Kubernetes) or a11y (for accessibility)

### [נֶפֶשׁ (nephesh)](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/%D7%A0%D7%A4%D7%A9)
The soul, the state of being alive.

### [木漏れ日 (komorebi)](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/%E6%9C%A8%E6%BC%8F%E3%82%8C%E6%97%A5)
The effect of sunlight filtering through the leaves of trees.

### [余裕 (yoyū)](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/%e4%bd%99%e8%a3%95)
Having something enough, in abundance.
