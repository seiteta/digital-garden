Learning a skill requires practice.
It might even be the only way to learn anything.
How would you learn to write without pracicing it with your hand and a sheet of paper?
How would you learn to meditate without sitting for hours, watching your breath?

But practice can be transferred.
When someone has walked the walk, they can precipitate their learning in a transferrable form.
They can write or tell something that will unlock the learner or help them learn faster.
Teachers can allow learner to go faster because they don't have to redo everything from scratch.
Knowledge compounds. One of the reasons why our species dominates its home planet.

Practice is also needed in machine learning. 
The models that had been ruling the field for the last decade are based on neural networks trained on millions of examples.
Models try stuff, we tell them if they succeed or not, update their behavior, over and over again. Practice.
More clever methods were tried before that, but brute-forcing learning with practice won in the end.
