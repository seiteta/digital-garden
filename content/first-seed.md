Publishing the first article on a website is always the scariest.
It's even worse than writing those first words on a brand-new notebook. Commits are public. They stay forever.
In those moments, I remind myself that nothing beats action. Write, edit, publish, don’t overthink it.

My intention with this new space is to grow a [digital garden](https://maggieappleton.com/garden-history).
Gardens are alive. Always changing. So maybe I’ll edit this article a hundred times.
Or maybe I’ll prune it one morning. But it has to be published first.

As they say:
> The best time to publish your first article was 20 years ago. The second best time is now.
